ASAPPlanner Stage Viewer

The planner's four stages for one workload: the frontends lower the queries into one logical DAG (Stage 0), Pass 1 and Pass 2 propose logical ASAP candidates (Stage 1), each becomes physical plans that differ in what runs at ingestion time (Stage 2), and Stage 3 checks accuracy, latency and deployment capabilities, prices every valid plan and selects the cheapest.
